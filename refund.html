2. UK and EU Consumer Rights Law

In accordance with the UK Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the EU Consumer Rights Directive, you have a statutory right to cancel a digital service contract within 14 days of purchase ("cooling-off period").

  • Normally, accessing digital content immediately waives this statutory right. However, PromedAI voluntarily extends this right to all users, providing a full 30-day refund window regardless of usage.
